What is a Wildcard Mask?

A wildcard mask is the inverse of a subnet mask. Each subnet-mask bit is reversed to calculate the corresponding wildcard-mask bit.

Subnet Mask: 255.255.255.0

Wildcard Mask: 0.0.0.255

Common Use

Wildcard masks are commonly used in networking configurations such as Cisco ACLs and routing protocols.

Example

For: 192.168.10.0/24

Subnet Mask: 255.255.255.0

Wildcard Mask: 0.0.0.255
